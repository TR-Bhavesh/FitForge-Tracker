🎯 What is FitForge?

FitForge Tracker is a complete fitness tracking solution that helps you:

  • Track Your Diet - Log meals with 60+ foods in the database
  • Monitor Workouts - Track exercises, calculate calories burned
  • Visualize Progress - See your body change with live body visualization
  • Analyze Data - Beautiful charts and statistics
  • Stay Private - All data stored locally on your device

🚀 Getting Started

1 Create Your Account
  • Click "Launch App" button above
  • Click "Sign up" on the login page
  • Enter your details: name, email, username, password
  • Add your stats: age, gender, height (cm), weight (lbs)
2 Set Your Goals
  • Go to Dashboard after login
  • Use the BMI Calculator to check your current status
  • Use the Calorie Calculator to set daily goals
  • Choose your activity level and goal (weight loss/gain/maintain)
3 Start Tracking
  • Diet Tracker: Log every meal - search from database or add custom food
  • Workout Tracker: Log exercises - use templates or create your own
  • Water Tracker: Quick-add water intake throughout the day
  • Daily Activity: Track steps and active minutes
4 Monitor Progress
  • Check Dashboard daily for quick stats
  • View body visualization to see how you look
  • Go to Analytics for detailed charts and graphs
  • Log weight regularly to track changes
5 Stay Consistent
  • Log meals immediately after eating
  • Track workouts as you complete them
  • Check your streak on Analytics page
  • Unlock milestones and achievements!

🎨 Key Features at a Glance

🧍 Body Visualization

Your dashboard shows a live body representation that changes based on your BMI:

  • Blue (Thin): Underweight (BMI < 18.5)
  • Purple (Athletic): Normal weight with muscle definition (BMI 18.5-24.9)
  • Orange (Fuller): Overweight (BMI 25-29.9)
  • Red (Larger): Obese (BMI 30+)

Update your weight anytime to see changes!

🍽️ Smart Food Tracking

Comprehensive food database includes:

  • Indian foods: Dosa, Rice, Dal, Curry, Biryani, etc.
  • Proteins: Eggs, Chicken, Fish, Paneer, Tofu
  • Quick combos: Rice+Curry, Dosa+Chutney, Eggs+OJ
  • Complete nutrition: Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fats

🏋️ Workout Templates

Pre-made workout routines save time:

  • Chest + Triceps (4 exercises, 60 min)
  • Back + Biceps (4 exercises, 60 min)
  • Leg Day (5 exercises, 65 min)
  • Shoulders + Core (5 exercises, 55 min)
  • Cardio Blast (3 exercises, 45 min)
  • Full Body (6 exercises, 70 min)

Each template auto-calculates calories burned!

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Track your progress with interactive charts:

  • Weight progress over time
  • Calorie intake vs burned comparison
  • Exercise distribution by category
  • Macronutrient breakdown
  • Daily protein intake trends
  • Weekly activity calendar

💡 Pro Tips

✅ For Best Results:
  • Log meals immediately - don't wait until end of day
  • Track water intake - aim for 3-4 liters daily
  • Use workout templates to save time
  • Update weight weekly (same day, same time)
  • Check Analytics page for motivation
  • Export data monthly as backup
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Data is stored locally - clearing browser data will delete everything
  • Use "Export Data" feature regularly for backup
  • Each user account is separate - login with correct username
  • Charts require internet for Chart.js library

🎯 Example Daily Routine

Morning (6:00 AM):

  • Log breakfast: Search "Egg White" (4), "Orange Juice" (1)

Mid-Day (1:00 PM):

  • Quick add combo: "Dosa + Chutney"

  • Go to Workout Tracker
  • Load template: "Chest + Triceps" OR

  • Add cardio: "Treadmill Incline Walk" (30 min)

Dinner (8:15 PM):

  • Log meal: Egg whites (4), Dosa (1)
  • Add water: 300ml

Night (10:00 PM):

  • Check Dashboard - view today's totals
  • Update body stats if weight changed
  • Check Analytics for weekly progress

  • Click Save
